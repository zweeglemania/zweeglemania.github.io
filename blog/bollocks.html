February

  • February 1, 2026: A member of LEAKED named Coming of Jesus leaks an unfinished, one-hour long work-print of The Paper Kingdom online. Coming of Jesus's account is banned shortly after for (at the time) unknown reasons, though forum users alleged that he was scamming o.

    Link: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 6, 2026: A user named Thunder of Zeus leaks a track titled "Don't You Get Behind That Bus!", theorized by the community to be an unreleased Gerard Way solo track (though Content ID lists it as My Chemical Romance).

    In a later conversation with a forum user, Thunder of Zeus reveals he obtained the track from a anonymous seller—and that he leaked it for fun.

    Link: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 7, 2026: A user named InpuT leaks a track titled "Into Your Arms", following a week of community speculation over it's absence from the initial work-print leak. InpuT accuses an unidentified individual (later revealed to be Coming of Jesus) of double selling and scamming.

    Link: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 11, 2026: Coming of Jesus returns on a new account, and provides snippets of demos for the songs "Dive" and "High Hopes", which he originally provided to organizers of a failed group buy, whom he accused of "wasting my time".

    He claims to have offered lossless files, demos, alternate versions, and new unreleased material to the organizers of the failed group buy, and says he will now be accepting private offers for the material.

    Link: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 12, 2026: Coming of Jesus leaks another track titled "Gaway Song", and says he was told that it's the "only other leaked track in circulation". He re-reiterates that he has multiple unreleased tracks, including demos and lossless files for The Paper Kingdom, and that he's still open to private offers or group buys.

    He would later claim that all of the files have been sold to a private seller, though he would contradict himself many times after this statement.

    Later that day, Coming of Jesus leaks a demo for the song "Dogs", and credits forum user Galileeo11 for buying the demo and allowing it to be released to the community, before claiming all other files have been sold.

    Links: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 12, 2026: In a now deleted post, forum member Disoveries22 leaks the following three tracks:

    • An alternate version of "Bike Thief", with it not including the "Stars Align" intro present in the initial leak, and a different outro.
    • An alternate version of "War Beneath The Rain", which doesn't transition into the song "Paper Swords" at the end, unlike in the initial leak.
    • A lossless version of the initial leak, which is later proven to be fake.

    They say Coming of Jesus is a known doxxer and scammer, who extorted Excalibur out of thousands of dollars alongside the album itself. They also allege that Coming of Jesus wanted to sell "insanely private" information about the band to private buyers, and that his toxic behaviour in the community is well known.

    Links: Unavailable (Post Deleted)

  • February 14, 2026: Coming of Jesus calls out one of his buyers for not vaulting the sold material they had allegedly agreed upon, and leaks two more unreleased files as retaliation—a track titled "BATMAN MAP DEMO" and another file called "Sketchs in Batman Map", a twenty-minute audio recording of Gerard and Ray working on a song together in the studio.

    Link: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 16, 2026: Coming of Jesus releases another audio recording titled "Batman Map (Idea Session)", claiming that a snippet of it had been circulating previously.

    In the replies, he addresses rumours about files from 2019, claiming that they're false, and any files dated 2019 came from Doug re-exporting old files from 2012 while revisiting old material with the band.

    Links: Unavailable due to legal reasons.

  • February 17, 2026: LEAKED member Bebsy leaks a demo of the band's 2022 single The Foundations of Decay, and says it's from March 2022.

    Links: Unavailable due to legal reasons.
